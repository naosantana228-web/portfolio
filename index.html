Effectiveness & impact

A score is only useful when its claim is clear.

Assessment asks: What does the learner know or demonstrate? Evaluation asks: Did the learning solution achieve its intended result? A test score is one signal, not the whole evaluation. The strength of the claim determines the evidence required.

Decision rules

Three principles that protect delivery quality.

01

Diagnose before design

If the barrier is workflow, tools, ownership, feedback or capacity, change that system. Use learning for capability, practice, judgement and reinforcement.

02

Reduce scope before standards

On a fixed deadline, protect the required outcome, critical practice, accuracy, accessibility and launch risk; move lower-value polish into measured iteration.

03

Match evidence to the claim

A test score, survey count, course completion and business measure answer different questions. Do not let one signal make a stronger claim than it can support.
